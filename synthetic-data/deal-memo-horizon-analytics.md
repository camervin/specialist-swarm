# Deal Memo — Project Horizon

**Target:** Horizon Analytics Ltd
**Acquirer:** BTS-Synthetic Capital (internal codename: Project Horizon)
**Deal type:** Acquisition (100% equity purchase)
**Proposed enterprise value:** $48M (6× ARR)
**Date:** 2026-05-01
**Prepared by:** M&A Team — BTS-Synthetic

---

## 1. Target Overview

Horizon Analytics Ltd is a B2B SaaS company headquartered in Edinburgh, Scotland. Founded in 2018. Provides AI-driven analytics and reporting tools to mid-market financial services firms (insurance, wealth management, and banking).

- **ARR:** $8.0M (as of Q1 2026), growing ~35% YoY
- **Customers:** 62 paying customers (avg ACV ~$130K)
- **Employees:** 94 FTE (58 engineering & product, 22 GTM, 14 G&A)
- **Net Revenue Retention:** 118%
- **Gross margin:** 72%
- **Burn:** ~$400K/month (18 months runway at current cash)

### Key products
1. **HorizonView** — self-service analytics dashboards for compliance and risk reporting (60% of ARR)
2. **HorizonAI** — an LLM-powered report generation module launched in Q3 2025 (40% of ARR, fastest-growing)

---

## 2. Strategic Rationale

1. **Expand into financial services vertical.** Horizon's customer base gives BTS-Synthetic an immediate foothold in regulated-industry analytics — a segment we've struggled to penetrate organically.
2. **AI-native product.** HorizonAI is built on a modern LLM stack (Anthropic Claude, LangChain, pgvector). Acqui-hire of ML talent would accelerate BTS-Synthetic's own AI roadmap by 12–18 months.
3. **Cross-sell opportunity.** 40 of Horizon's 62 customers are already in BTS-Synthetic's target ICP; immediate upsell surface for our core data platform.

## 5. Known Issues / Red Flags

1. **Founder departure:** CTO and co-founder Priya Sharma has indicated she intends to leave 12 months post-close. She is the lead architect of HorizonAI.
2. **Customer concentration:** Top 3 customers represent 38% of ARR. Losing any one is a material event.
3. **Series B terms:** The Series B preferred shares (held by Vertex Ventures) include a 2× liquidation preference. At $48M EV, common shareholders would receive ~$12M after preference stack.
4. **GDPR exposure:** Horizon processes personal data of EU banking customers. Data processing agreements with 11 customers have not been reviewed since GDPR updates in 2023.
5. **Open-source licensing:** HorizonAI uses several AGPL-licensed libraries. This may require a licensing audit before the product is redistributed or embedded.

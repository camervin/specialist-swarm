## Notes

1. **Revenue recognition:** Subscription revenue recognised ratably. Professional services (~8% of revenue) recognised on completion.
2. **Deferred revenue ($3.1M):** Primarily annual contracts billed upfront. This is a positive indicator of cash collection quality.
3. **R&D capitalisation:** $890K of R&D costs capitalised in 2025 (up from $320K in 2024). Auditors flagged for disclosure; policy is compliant with UK GAAP FRS 102.
4. **EBITDA worsening:** Losses widening due to planned GTM expansion (7 AEs hired in 2025) and HorizonAI infrastructure costs (GPU compute). Management guidance is for continued investment through 2026.
